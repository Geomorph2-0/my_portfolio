## Overview

A custom-designed flight controller architected from the ground up to support both brushed and brushless motor configurations. This is my final year project at Redeemer's University, targeting a complete alternative to off-the-shelf flight controllers like Betaflight/Pixhawk. Conceptualized in April 2025, with active implementation beginning in November 2025.

## Problem

The price-to-performance ratio of commercial flight controllers is unfavourable for Nigerian education and research contexts. The goal was to lead development of a locally reproducible alternative targeting comparable performance at significantly lower cost.

## Architecture

The key trade-off was separating the real-time control layer (STM32) from the telemetry layer (ESP32) to meet performance constraints without compromising reliability — keeping flight-critical PID loops isolated from the overhead of wireless communication.

## Implementation

**Firmware**
- Developed in C using STM32Cube Suite and C++ in Arduino.
- PID control loops for roll, pitch, and yaw stabilization
- Sensor fusion algorithms combining gyroscope and accelerometer data for real-time attitude estimation
- Motor mixing algorithms for quadcopter geometry
- Capable of attitude hold

**Hardware**
- Custom veroboard designed and iterated upon
- STM32 microcontroller at the core of real time flight operations and ESP32 microcontroller for telemetry delivery and GCS (Ground Control Station) communication
- ESC signal output for brushed and brushless motor control (PWM / DSHOT)
- IMU integration (accelerometer + gyroscope)
- Telemetry viewing via a web-based dashboard
- Support for standard RC transmitter input (FlySky i6X and iA10B/iA6/iA6B receiver) via iBUS

**Additional Features (In Progress)**
- Remote control via Wi-Fi and cellular networks
- Waypoint mapping and autonomous flight modes
- Position hold via GPS

## Outcome

Validated core requirements in live testing: achieved over 10 minutes of stable flight and 1ms telemetry delivery to ground station. The next defined evaluation milestone is benchmarking against the Matek F411 WTE.
