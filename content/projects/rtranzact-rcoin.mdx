## Overview

RTranzact is an offline campus payment system built to enable monetary transactions over a local WiFi network — no internet connection required. The system introduces RCoin as its internal currency, allowing students and campus users to transact peer-to-peer within the network. Built solo under the team name **MB-Zero** for the Interswitch Discovery Series at Redeemer's University in September 2025.

## Problem

Campus transactions — canteen payments, peer transfers, event ticketing — depend entirely on internet connectivity or cash. On a campus where network availability can be unreliable, this creates friction. The goal was to build a payment layer that lives entirely on the local network, making transactions instant, offline-capable, and independent of external infrastructure.

## Implementation

- **Transport layer**: Transactions routed entirely over local WiFi — no external API calls, no internet dependency
- **Currency**: RCoin — a custom digital token scoped to the campus network, used as the medium of exchange
- **Architecture**: Peer-to-peer transaction model with a local server handling ledger state and balance reconciliation
- **Use cases targeted**: Canteen payments, peer-to-peer transfers, and general campus commerce

## Event

Built over **three days** at the **Interswitch Discovery Series**, a hackathon hosted at Redeemer's University in September 2025. Competed solo under the team name **MB-Zero** and placed **second overall** in the hackathon. Interswitch subsequently extended an invitation to develop RTranzact further.
